# District Field Notes

Anonymised evidence from programme delivery, institutional interaction and entrepreneur journeys.

## What belongs here

- Field observations
- Process bottlenecks
- Entrepreneur journey summaries
- Institutional coordination problems
- Assisted-access and digital-literacy issues
- Short cases showing where delivery succeeded or failed

## Field-note structure

- Date and district
- Programme or service journey
- Stakeholders involved
- Expected process
- Observed process
- Point of friction or dropout
- Consequence for the entrepreneur
- Possible explanation
- Evidence still required
- Potential system-level response

## Safeguards

Do not upload Aadhaar numbers, phone numbers, bank details, confidential programme records or personally identifiable beneficiary data. Obtain consent before publishing attributable information. Use codes or composite cases where appropriate.

## First output

Document five anonymised cases covering the path from training to formalisation, finance, mentoring or market transactions.
