# Applied Research

District diagnostics, implementation studies and evidence-based case studies.

## Initial flagship study

**From Training to Enterprise Outcomes: Mapping the Entrepreneurship-Support Journey in a District of Eastern Uttar Pradesh**

### Central question

Where do entrepreneurs lose continuity between mobilisation, training, registration, mentoring, finance, market access and post-programme monitoring?

### Planned components

- Research question and scope
- Literature and programme review
- Stakeholder map
- Interview guide
- Entrepreneur journey evidence
- Process and leakage analysis
- Findings and limitations
- Actionable recommendations

## Evidence standard

Clearly separate observed facts, participant perspectives, administrative data, interpretation and recommendation. Record limitations and avoid claiming causality without sufficient evidence.
