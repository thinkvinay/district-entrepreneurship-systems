# Pilot Evidence

Plans and findings from small, measurable tests of district entrepreneurship-support improvements.

## Pilot package

Each pilot should include:

- Problem statement
- Target group and district
- Baseline
- Theory of change
- Institutional roles
- Implementation plan
- Outcome indicators
- Data-protection safeguards
- Risk register
- Review schedule
- Results and lessons
- Replication conditions

## Suggested initial indicators

- Formalisation completed
- Finance applications submitted and approved
- Market transactions—not onboarding alone
- Mentor interactions completed
- Grievances resolved
- Time required to access services
- Enterprise survival after six months
- Revenue or employment movement
- Participant dropout rate

Do not publish raw beneficiary data or confidential institutional records.
