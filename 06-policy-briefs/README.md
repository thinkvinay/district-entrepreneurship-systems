# Policy Briefs

Short recommendations that translate field evidence and system analysis into practical choices for public institutions.

## Standard format

1. Title stating the decision problem
2. Executive summary
3. Why the issue matters
4. Evidence from the district or programme
5. Institutional and digital-system diagnosis
6. Two or three feasible options
7. Recommended action
8. Implementation responsibility
9. Cost, risk and inclusion considerations
10. Measurable indicators

## Writing principles

- Address a specific institution or decision-maker.
- Use plain language.
- Distinguish evidence from inference.
- Recommend changes that can be implemented.
- Avoid proposing technology without process ownership.
- State limitations honestly.
