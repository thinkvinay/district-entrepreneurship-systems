# District Entrepreneurship Systems Lab

A working repository for studying how India's existing institutions and digital public infrastructure can produce measurable entrepreneurship and MSME outcomes at district level.

## Purpose

This repository documents my transition from entrepreneurship-programme implementation into applied digital governance. It brings together field observations, structured research, service-system designs, pilot evidence and policy recommendations.

My central question is:

> How can district institutions connect entrepreneurs with learning, mentoring, formalisation, finance, markets, government schemes and continued support through existing digital public infrastructure?

## Working principles

1. Problem first, technology second.
2. Improve existing systems before proposing another portal.
3. Use the district as the unit of experimentation.
4. Measure completed journeys and economic outcomes—not registrations alone.
5. Test on a small scale before recommending expansion.
6. Design for assisted and offline access as well as digital access.
7. Protect personal data and preserve public accountability.

## Initial research direction

**Working study:** *From Training to Enterprise Outcomes: Mapping the Entrepreneurship-Support Journey in a District of Eastern Uttar Pradesh*

The first study will examine where entrepreneurs lose continuity between mobilisation, training, registration, mentoring, finance, market access and post-programme support.
